Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and ordering process

What is the typical turnaround time for custom orders?

Custom bespoke pieces typically require 4-8 weeks from initial consultation to delivery. This allows our master artisans to source premium materials and hand-craft your garment to perfection. Rush orders may be available upon request.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship worldwide to over 50 countries. International orders are subject to customs duties and taxes which vary by country. We provide full tracking and insurance on all shipments. Contact us for shipping quotes and delivery estimates.

Can I schedule a private styling consultation?

Absolutely! We offer personalized styling sessions both in-studio and virtually. Our consultants will help you curate pieces that match your style, body type, and lifestyle. Contact us to book your consultation today.

What is your return and exchange policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for items in original condition with tags attached. Custom and tailored pieces are final sales. Exchanges are available for size adjustments. Please contact our customer service team for details.

Do you collaborate with brands or influencers?

We're always open to exciting collaboration opportunities. If you're interested in partnering with Nexa Fashion House, please send us an email with your proposal to divineigwesi1184@gmail.com.
